Secure by default

Hardening is part of the design, not an add-on.

  • Short-lived JWT access tokens with rotating refresh cookies
  • Default-deny routes, rate limiting and account lockout
  • Email verification and account deletion with a grace period
  • Hardened containers behind Caddy with automatic HTTPS
  • Available in English and Czech. Emails and notifications use each user's saved language.

Self-host in minutes

Run it on a small VPS with 2 to 4 GB of RAM. Prebuilt container images, one shared Caddy for HTTPS, MariaDB on an internal network, plus backup and restore scripts.
